About

I like to know where the alert came from.

I'm at the start of my career in security. Here is what I've done so far, how I work, and what I'm looking for.

The short version

I graduated in 2026 with a B.Tech in Computer Science (Cybersecurity) from Quantum University, Roorkee. From April to July 2026 I was a Threat Research Intern at Seqrite Labs, the research arm of Quick Heal Technologies, where I built an automated IOC validation pipeline across VirusTotal, AbuseIPDB, OTX and ThreatFox, automated STIX 2.1 bundles for OpenCTI, and modelled an APT28 tracking profile.

Alongside my degree and internship I built Sigma Generator, an open-source tool that turns MITRE ATT&CK into graded Sigma rules and correlation rules. It is the best evidence of how I think.

How I work

  • I want to understand the whole path. Where does the log come from, which field holds the evidence, why did this rule fire, and what should the analyst check first.
  • I measure my own work. When I audited the first version of my rule generator, 27% of its rules were useless. I removed them and wrote down why.
  • My tool says “no rule” when it has nothing solid. It skips 66 techniques instead of writing filler, because a rule nobody can justify is noise.
  • I document as I go. Case notes, findings and handoffs are part of the job, not an afterthought.

What I am looking for

A first full-time role, ideally as a SOC analyst. That is where most of my practice is. I am also open to threat intelligence, detection engineering, incident response and security testing roles, and I would rather join a good team and learn than hold out for one job title. I am based in Pune, open to relocating anywhere in India or working remotely, fine with 24x7 rotational shifts, and able to join immediately.

Certificates and courses

Cybersecurity Essentials (Cisco Networking Academy), Introduction to CyberOps and Next-Gen Network Security (IT Masters / Charles Sturt University), Hands-On Penetration Testing with Metasploit (Infosys Springboard), Digital Forensics Basics (Cybrary) and Email Security Associate (OPSWAT Academy).
